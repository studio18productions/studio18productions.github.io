Questions, answered.

Do you travel to our location?
Yes. On site is how we work. We bring lighting, backdrops and cameras to your office, store, warehouse or event, so your photos and video show your real space and people.
What's the difference between video and AI video?
Video production is a real shoot with a crew and cinema cameras. AI video generates visuals, voices and scenes with AI tools, then our editors direct and finish them. Many projects mix both: a real shoot for the hero footage, AI for B-roll, explainers and language versions.
Which AI should my business use?
It depends on your tools and your work. Teams on Google Workspace often start with Gemini. Teams doing heavy writing, documents and coding tend to prefer Claude. ChatGPT is a strong all-rounder. We recommend a mix after a short discovery call, and we can set up more than one.
What does AI training look like?
Hands-on sessions with your team, on your actual work. We set up accounts, build the prompts and workflows you'll use daily, and train in person or over video. You also get written guides to keep.
How fast can you deliver?
Photography is usually delivered within a week of the shoot. Video depends on length and scope. AI video and AI setups often land in days. We confirm timelines before any work starts.
How do I get a quote?
Send a message below with what you need. We reply within one business day with a short call to scope it, then a clear written quote.
